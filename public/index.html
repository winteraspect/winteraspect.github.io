About Me

I am an economist and researcher specializing in Financial Economics, Market Design, and International Finance. I am currently a PhD Candidate in Economics at the University of Technology Sydney (UTS), expecting to graduate in H2 2026.

Prior to my academic pursuit at UTS, I served as a Senior Economist at RICS (Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors) for the Asia-Pacific (APAC) region, based in Singapore. There, I led macroeconomic research, managed global market sentiment surveys (such as the Commercial Property and Construction Monitors), and provided thought leadership on property markets, real estate dynamics, and infrastructure investments.

🔍 Research Interests

  • Financial Economics: Market microstructure, asset pricing, systemic risk, and property/housing finance.
  • Market Design: Auction theory, matching markets, resource allocation mechanisms, and policy evaluation.
  • International Finance: Capital flows, global macroeconomic policy, and real estate investment trusts (REITs).
