About Donglai Luo

I am a PhD Candidate in Economics at the University of Technology Sydney (UTS), specializing in Financial Economics, Market Design, and International Finance. My research addresses market microstructure, auction mechanism design, and international property/housing finance systems.

Alongside my academic training, I have a background in professional policy and industry research, having spent years as a Senior Economist at the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors (RICS) APAC region, where I led sentiment analysis and economic forecasting for real estate and construction sectors.

📝 Research & Publications

Published Academic Papers

  1. “Istanbul Flower Auction: The Need for Speed” (with Isa Hafalir, Onur Kesten, Katerina Sherstyuk, and Cong Tao)
    Games and Economic Behavior, 2026.
    Abstract: We investigate speed-related issues and bidding behaviors in the Istanbul Flower Auction, highlighting mechanical design limits and bidders' reaction speeds in Dutch auctions.
  2. “When Speed is of Essence: Perishable Goods Auctions” (with Onur Kesten, Katerina Sherstyuk, and Cong Tao)
    Journal of Economic Theory, 2026.
    Abstract: This paper models multi-unit Dutch auctions for highly perishable goods, establishing equilibrium bidding behaviors and comparing market efficiency against standard formats.
