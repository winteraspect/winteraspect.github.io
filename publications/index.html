Co-Authored Academic Publications

  1. “Istanbul Flower Auction: The Need for Speed”
    Co-authors: Isa Hafalir, Onur Kesten, Katerina Sherstyuk, and Cong Tao.
    Journal: Games and Economic Behavior (2026).
    Links: Journal DOI | arXiv Preprint
    Abstract: We investigate speed-related issues and bidding behaviors in the Istanbul Flower Auction, highlighting mechanical design limits and bidders' reaction speeds in Dutch auctions.

Working Papers & Work-in-Progress

  • “Non-linear Pricing with Resale”
    Co-authors: Isa E. Hafalir and Hadi Yektas.
    Status: Working Paper / Research Project.
    Abstract: We investigate the optimal non-linear pricing strategy for a monopolist when consumers have opportunities to resell purchased goods to each other in a secondary market.

  • “Cross-border carbon pricing and international trade” (2025)
    Role: Author (Work-in-progress).
    Abstract: Using a computational general equilibrium (CGE) model, we study the impact of carbon pricing under common but differentiated responsibilities (CBDR) and trade policies on global emissions, economic welfare, and industry competitiveness.
